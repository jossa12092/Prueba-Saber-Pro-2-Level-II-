Before you start

  1. Read the passage and photographs carefully. The vocabulary matching part includes five definitions together; the other parts display one question at a time. The seven parts may appear in any order, but the questions within each part stay in order.
  2. Choose an option and press Seleccionar respuesta to lock it. A confirmed answer cannot be changed.
  3. You have 90 minutes from the moment you start.
  4. Your answers, selected options, current question, and remaining time are saved automatically in this device and browser. When you reopen or refresh the same exam link, it will return to where you were. If the test is locked because you leave the tab or press a restricted key, the question open at that moment is automatically marked incorrect (0 points), and the teacher must unlock the test.
  5. After submitting, you will see a 0,0–5,0 grade and your attempt will be closed. Tap Send result to teacher to send the PDF directly to the teacher. You cannot restart it from this browser.

Use the same browser and device when returning. Do not use private browsing or clear site data. A local browser file cannot prevent a new attempt from a different browser or device. The 90-minute timer does not pause when you leave. Translation, copying and printing controls are enabled. This is a classroom practice test, not an official ICFES exam.
